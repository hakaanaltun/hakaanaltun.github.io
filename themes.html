Themes

Sixteen ways the site can look—thirteen palettes for the day, two darks for the night, and one that takes its colours from the sun. Pick one and it is on at once. What you choose here stays on this device until you change your mind.

With nothing chosen, the site follows the sky. Light uses White & Slate. Choose System in the footer menu to follow your device’s light or dark preference.
