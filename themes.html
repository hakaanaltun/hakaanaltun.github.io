Themes

Fifteen ways the site can look—twelve palettes for the day, two darks for the night, and one that takes its colours from the sun. Pick one and it is on at once. What you choose here stays on this device until you change your mind.

With nothing chosen, the site follows the device it is read on: its own Ash & Gold by day, Dark at night. That rule is System, the one thing in the footer menu with nothing to show here—it is not a look, but whatever the device says.
