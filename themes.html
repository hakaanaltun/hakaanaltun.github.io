Themes

Fifteen ways the site can look—twelve palettes for the day, two darks for the night, and one that takes its colours from the sun. Pick one and it is on at once. What you choose here stays on this device until you change your mind.

The default is System: White & Slate in your device’s light mode and Dark in dark mode. Choose any palette or Follow the sky to keep that choice on this device.
