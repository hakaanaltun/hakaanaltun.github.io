Themes

Fourteen ways the site can look—twelve palettes for the day, and the two darks for the night. Pick one and it is on at once. What you choose here stays on this device until you change your mind.

With nothing chosen, the site follows the device it is read on: its own Ash & Gold by day, Dark at night. The footer menu keeps two more that are rules rather than looks—System, and Follow the sky, where the paper stops being paper and becomes the sky over your city, moving with the sun all day rather than switching at it.
