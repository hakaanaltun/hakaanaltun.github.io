# The Space art direction

Three connected scene illustrations generated with the built-in image_gen tool.
The canonical Farm House upstairs watercolor was the style reference; the first space scene supplied continuity for the following views.
Only WebP compression was applied after generation. Each asset is 1536 × 1024.

## earth-vicinity-watercolor

Saved asset: `pictures/space/assets/earth-vicinity-watercolor.webp`

Final prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one landscape 3:2 watercolor painting, 1536x1024. Input image 1 is STYLE ONLY: match its broad transparent watercolor strokes, restrained 3-4 values, visible delicate pencil, exposed ivory cold-press paper edges, occasional backruns and loosely finished regions; do not copy room subjects. Scene: Earth vicinity in spare deep indigo navy space wash with very few stars and irregular exposed paper edges. Earth globe center (36% width,55% height), diameter 48% image height; recognizable Africa center, Europe above, Arabia upper right, blue oceans, loose white clouds and subtle atmosphere rim. Moon smaller globe center (78%,35%), diameter14% image height, gray lunar mare and highlands. Small recognizable real ISS center(64%,71%): long central truss with paired rectangular solar panel wings and white pressurized modules at center, no rocket forms. All sunlight FROM UPPER LEFT; restrained pale cobalt ocean, muted ochre olive land, ivory cloud, gray moon, navy wash. Painting first, qualitative scientific interpretation with artistically compressed distances, separate celestial bodies not adjacent planet sizes. No Mars, extra planets, text, labels, UI, arrows, glossy digital art, cartoon or photoreal texture carpet. Leave loose washes and breathing room, real watercolor on ivory paper.
