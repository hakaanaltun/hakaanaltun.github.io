# The Space art direction

Three connected scene illustrations generated with the built-in image_gen tool.
The canonical Farm House upstairs watercolor was the style reference; the first space scene supplied continuity for the following views.
Only WebP compression was applied after generation. Each asset is 1536 × 1024.

## earth-vicinity-wide-watercolor

Saved asset: `pictures/space/assets/earth-vicinity-wide-watercolor.webp`

The current opening view was recomposed with the built-in image_gen tool using the previous vicinity illustration as the edit target and Near Earth as the continuity reference. Earth's diameter is approximately 290 pixels in this opening and 880 pixels in the closer view.

Earth center pixel(507,532), center(33%,52%); whole circle contained approximately x317..697 y342..722. Earth occupies only ~7% of total canvas area, absolutely less than one sixth. Earth must look small and distant within vast empty space; do NOT enlarge it to fill the canvas. ISS center pixel(952,696),center(62%,68%),ONLY150px overall width, realistic long central truss, paired rectangular solar arrays, white central pressurized modules; retain broad structural resemblance, qualitative enlargement for visibility. All objects separated by ample navy wash; reduce existing stars to just a very few. No additional objects, no Mars, no extra planets, no labels, text, UI, arrows, lines, fantasy lights, cartoon, glossy digital rendering or photoreal fine-detail carpet.
