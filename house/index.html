The room is drawn here; what is in it comes from /house/catalog.json (house/catalog.json), which js/house.js reads to put out the day's words, question and lines. Everything a reader keeps goes through js/keep.js, which the other pages use too, so the drawer is the same drawer wherever a thing was kept. "Today" is İstanbul's day and the light is İstanbul's light: the house is there, and every reader sees the same room on the same day. {%- endcomment -%}
