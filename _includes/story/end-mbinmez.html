His mother would be at the corner by first light and stay until it was over. He would arrive too late to stand beside her while the block came down. He could see no point in going now.

He took the train to his corner and walked the five minutes home. He collected the key from the neighbor across the landing. The tree at the corner stood in new leaf.

He put the kettle on. The record was where he had left it.

The 4:15 left with his seat empty.

At first light the phone lay on the table, face up. He made no call, and none came.

The machines started on the far side. He could not hear them.
