{%- comment -%} Search leads the strip as a field rather than a glyph. It spent a release as one of four unlabelled icons at the far right — and on a phone portrait inside the dropdown behind the chevron, two taps from a reader who had already gone looking for it. A bar names itself without being opened. Everywhere but /search/. The strip is the band of elsewhere — the feed, the two addresses, the instruments, the wardrobe, the share sheet — and on the search page the bar would be the one thing on it pointing at the page under the reader's feet. The icon it replaced said as much with aria-current="page". The page's own field is also the better of the two: site-search.js binds that one, so it answers live as you type, while this one posts and reloads. Offering the weaker copy of a control at the moment the real one is on screen is not a second chance, it is a second box.
