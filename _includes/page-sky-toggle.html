{% comment %} page-sky-toggle.html — the corner theme button's third state. The instruments with a bare chrome bar carry one button that used to hold two states, light and dark. This gives it a third, "follow the sky", and the note that says whose sky: the mode picks a city from the browser's time zone, so a page that follows the sun without naming the place is telling the reader half of it. The note exists only while the sky is on, so for everyone who stays on light or dark the chrome is exactly what it was. It sits in the chrome bar just left of the buttons, taken out of flow so the chrome's three-column grid — 1fr auto 1fr, with the masthead centred in the middle — cannot be pushed off centre by a long city name; and being inside the chrome, the pages with an immersive mode hide it along with everything else. Under 820px the bar has no room left of the buttons before the masthead, so the note steps aside there and the button's own label carries the city. Requires page-sky.html in the head (window.OLAE_PAGE_SKY) and, in the markup, .actions > button#themeBtn carrying .icon-moon, .icon-sun and .icon-sky. /read/ and /write/ do not use this: they reach the same three states through their shelf. {% endcomment %}
