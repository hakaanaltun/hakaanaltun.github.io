Before the Word

The cold came in through the mouth of the cave and crawled across the floor, looking for something living. We had nothing to give it. We had only one another.

The fire had gone out.

There was a body next to mine. In the dark, all bodies were one body.

A hand found my shoulder. It rested there, already at home. I touched the hand. The hand did not move away.

A child in the cluster made a small sound, something between breath and asking. The woman beside it pulled the child closer. The sound stopped.

Around us, bodies shifted, and a forehead came to rest against a back. Heat moved from one body into another until the whole cluster breathed in almost the same rhythm.

Outside, the wind worried at the mouth of the cave. Inside, we held.
