The Traitor Within

We had built castles in the dark.

The holdings became stars. The stars became fires. We had pushed spears of light into the cold, and the cold backed away, and around us there was warmth, and order, and the deep slow burn of something that finally lasted.

The light came from inside us. Each photon we gave to the dark was a piece of us.

The center of one of our stars fell inward; the force that held it had forgotten why. We were drawn after it, pressed closer together, until a shock passed through us and drove the outer layers back.

Those of us carried outward kept moving. For millions of years we had been held close together. Now the distances between us were growing.

Other stars were still burning. Their light passed through the places where ours had been.

We scattered. We carried carbon and iron into the dark. Much later, some of us would be in a cup of tea on a kitchen table at three in the morning.

And in scattering, we seeded.
