{%- comment -%} Every addressable thing on the site, as JSON records — the posts, the word stories, the instruments, the quizzes, the notes and the standing pages, in that order. Two files read this, and that is why it is an include rather than a loop written twice. /search-index.json needs the body of each record to search it. /link-preview.json needs only enough to show what is behind a link, and the bodies are what make the search index 114KB compressed, which is not a thing to fetch because a reader paused over a link. Same records, one of them carrying its text: {% include index-records.html body="true" %} Anything else — omitted, or any other value — leaves "text" out.
