{% comment %} site-sky.html — "Follow the sky" as a theme for the whole site. Where an instrument has four tokens, the site has a full block: grounds, four tiers of text, rules, the note wall, the section accent. This paints all of them from the two things the ramp gives — a ground and an ink mix — because a theme whose paper moves has to keep every relation to that paper moving with it. Anything fixed (icons, shadows, colour-scheme) is a plain block in css/style.css instead; only what depends on the sun is here. The tiers are alpha over the ground rather than free colours, so the hierarchy holds at every altitude instead of at one. Their contrast was solved against the deepened ramp before it was written: ink 7.4:1 at its worst, --vizon 6.2:1, which is where Dark's own dimmest text sits. Every section keeps its own hue, chroma and minimum lightness. The shared floor follows the ground, and style.css mixes the resulting base with 12% white once. The curve below was fitted against the final mixed accents across the whole sky ramp, including the writing’s cherry red. It keeps the accents above 5:1 while allowing deeper night tones. Every section and the theme preview read this same floor. The block is computed and returned before it is applied, because it has a second reader: the Follow the sky card on /themes/ is painted from the same tokens, so the card is not a drawing of the theme but the theme, at the minute it is looked at. Runs before first paint. js/theme.js drives it afterwards and re-paints every minute. {% endcomment %}
