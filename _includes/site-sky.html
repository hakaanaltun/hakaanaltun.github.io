{% comment %} site-sky.html — "Follow the sky" as a theme for the whole site. The sky has two halves, both in sky-ramp.html, which says why. From sunrise to sunset the theme is its day, a light paper with dark ink, set by the same rules as the twelve light palettes. From sunset to sunrise it is the night ramp, the deep navy and the violet of twilight, with light ink, and until nine in the evening the slate darkening into it. By day the painter sets , so every rule written for the light path applies, and data-sky="day"; css/palettes.css leaves a stored palette alone while data-sky is on. By night it sets data-theme="sky" and data-sky="night". js/theme.js, js/palettes.js and the search read data-sky to know the sky is the choice either way. The night: where an instrument has four tokens, the site has a full block: grounds, four tiers of text, rules, the note wall, the section accent. It paints all of them from the two things the ramp gives, a ground and an ink mix. The tiers are alpha over the ground, so the hierarchy holds at every altitude; ink is at least 6.5:1 and --vizon 5.5:1. Every section keeps its own hue, chroma and minimum lightness; the shared floor follows the ground, and style.css mixes the base with white once: 12% at the darkest of the night, rising with the header's words to 40% at the horizon. The block is computed and returned before it is applied, because it has a second reader: the Follow the sky card on /themes/ is painted from the same tokens, so the card is not a drawing of the theme but the theme, at the minute it is looked at. ?hour=13 stands the sun where it will be at that hour today, in the reader's own city (sky-ramp.html), so any hour can be looked at. Runs before first paint. js/theme.js drives it afterwards and re-paints every minute. {% endcomment %}
