{% comment %} site-sky.html — "Follow the sky" as a theme for the whole site. Where an instrument has four tokens, the site has a full block: grounds, four tiers of text, rules, the note wall, the section accent. This paints all of them from the two things the ramp gives — a ground and an ink mix — because a theme whose paper moves has to keep every relation to that paper moving with it. Anything fixed (icons, shadows, colour-scheme) is a plain block in css/style.css instead; only what depends on the sun is here. The tiers are alpha over the ground rather than free colours, so the hierarchy holds at every altitude instead of at one. Their contrast was solved against the deepened ramp before it was written: ink 7.4:1 at its worst, --vizon 6.2:1, which is where Dark's own dimmest text sits. The accent is the interesting one. The five section voices carry their own hue, chroma and night lightness in style.css, and on Dark's near-black ground those lightnesses are comfortable — the writing's red reads 6.20:1. On a lit sky ground the same value collapses to 2.73:1. So the sun sets a floor, --sky-ld-floor, and style.css takes max() of the voice's own value and the floor: at night every voice keeps exactly the lightness it was tuned to, and as the ground lifts the floor lifts under all five. The curve is 0.539 + 1.12·√L, clamped, fitted to sit just above the measured minimum at every altitude — on a dark ground a lighter accent is only ever more contrast, so the safe side of that curve is the high side. Worst case across all five voices and the whole ramp: 5.00:1. Runs before first paint. js/theme.js drives it afterwards and re-paints every minute. {% endcomment %}
