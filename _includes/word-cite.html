{%- comment -%} Footnote markers for one sentence, tying it to entries in the word's own `sources:` list. Without them a Sources block at the foot of the page reads as sourcing everything above it — the whole story rather than the one claim the sources actually cover. Usage, inside a word's body, right after the closing punctuation: {% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %} where the numbers are positions in that word's `sources:` list. The markers carry an aria-label, because a screen reader reading "one" out of the middle of a sentence is noise; "Source 1" is the thing meant. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign cite_numbers = include.n | split: "," -%} {% for cite_number in cite_numbers %}{{ cite_number | strip }}{% endfor %}
