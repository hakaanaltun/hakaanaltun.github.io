{%- comment -%} Footnote markers for one sentence, tying it to entries in the word's own `sources:` list. Without them a Sources block at the foot of the page reads as sourcing everything above it — the whole story rather than the one claim the sources actually cover. Usage, inside a word's body, right after the closing punctuation: {% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %} where the numbers are positions in that word's `sources:` list. The markers carry an aria-label, because a screen reader reading "one" out of the middle of a sentence is noise; "Source 1" is the thing meant. Each marker also carries the source itself, for the margin. On a wide enough screen the note sits out in the white space beside the line that earned it, so the reader can see who says so without leaving the sentence; below that width it is not rendered at all and the Sources block at the foot of the page is the only answer, exactly as it has always been. The margin copy is aria-hidden and holds no link. It says nothing the marker and the Sources block do not already say properly, and a link inside hidden content is focusable without being readable — the worst of both. The way to the source stays the numbered marker. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign cite_numbers = include.n | split: "," -%} {% for cite_number in cite_numbers %}{{ cite_number | strip }}{% endfor %}{%- if page.sources -%}{%- for cite_number in cite_numbers -%}{%- assign source_index = cite_number | strip | minus: 1 -%}{%- assign cited = page.sources[source_index] -%}{%- if cited -%}{{ cite_number | strip }}{{ cited.title }}{%- if cited.where -%}{{ cited.where }}{%- endif -%}{%- endif -%}{%- endfor -%}{%- endif -%}
