--- layout: default permalink: /words/bosh/ title: "bosh — Words with Stories" word: bosh pos: n. story: | Bosh means nonsense or empty talk in English. It comes from the Turkish word *boş*, meaning empty. It entered English in the nineteenth century and was helped along by J. J. Morier’s 1834 novel *Ayesha, the Maid of Kars*. What was empty in Turkish became empty talk in English, and then nonsense. It is old-fashioned now, but it is still there. *You’re talking bosh.* --- {% include word-story.html %}
