Sabotage is damage done on purpose to stop something working. It is a French
word, taken into English early in the twentieth century, from
<span lang="fr">*sabot*</span>, a wooden shoe or
clog.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

The story everybody knows is that workers threw their clogs into the machinery.
It is repeated everywhere and recorded nowhere: no account of anyone actually
doing it has been produced.{% include word-cite.html n="2" %}

The likelier route is duller and stays inside the language. French
<span lang="fr">*saboter*</span> meant to clatter about in wooden shoes, and
from there to work clumsily, and from there to botch a job on
purpose.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

The clog is in the word either way. Nobody can show that one was ever thrown
into a machine.{% include word-cite.html n="2" %}

*The line was shut down by sabotage.*
