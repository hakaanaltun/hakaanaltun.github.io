To consider something is to think about it carefully.

The word comes through Old French from Latin *considerare*, to observe
attentively or contemplate.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

A long-standing explanation connects the Latin verb with *sidus*, meaning a
star, heavenly body, or constellation. On that reading, *considerare* may once
have meant something like observing the stars closely, perhaps for navigation
or divination.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

The connection is probable rather than certain. Other explanations for the
Latin verb have been proposed, so the star story is best kept as a strong
possibility rather than a literal definition.{% include word-cite.html n="2" %}

English kept the idea of close attention even after the stars disappeared from
ordinary use.

*I need some time to consider the offer.*
