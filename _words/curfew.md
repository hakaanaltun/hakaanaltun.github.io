A curfew is an order to be off the streets after a certain hour. The word comes
from the Old French <span lang="fr">*cuevrefeu*</span>, cover
fire.{% include word-cite.html n="1" %}

In medieval towns built largely of timber, a bell rang in the evening and
households banked or covered their hearths for the night. A fire left untended
was everybody's problem.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

The cover itself was called a curfew too: a lid of terracotta or metal put over
the embers to hold them safely until morning.{% include word-cite.html n="2" %}

The bell stopped and the fire went out of the meaning. By the nineteenth
century the word had become what it is now, an hour after which you have to be
indoors.{% include word-cite.html n="1" %}

*The city was under curfew.*
