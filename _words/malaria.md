Malaria is a disease carried by mosquitoes. The name is the Italian
<span lang="it">*mala aria*</span>, bad air.{% include word-cite.html n="1" %}

It was named for what people believed caused it. Fevers were common around
marshes, and the vapour coming off stagnant water was held to be what made
people ill.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

The parasite was identified in 1880 and the mosquito was shown to carry it
before the century was out. The air had nothing to do with
it.{% include word-cite.html n="2" %}

The name did not change with the theory. A disease we now understand is still
called after an idea about air that nobody
holds.{% include word-cite.html n="1" %}

*Malaria is still one of the world's deadliest diseases.*
