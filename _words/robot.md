A robot is a machine that can carry out tasks automatically.

The word entered international use through Karel Čapek’s 1920 play *R.U.R.*,
where it named artificial workers. Čapek later credited his brother Josef with
suggesting the word.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

Josef drew it from Czech *robota*, a word connected with forced labour,
serf labour, or hard work. The labour was already in the name before the
modern machine existed.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

*The factory uses robots for welding.*
