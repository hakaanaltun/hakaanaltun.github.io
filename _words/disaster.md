A disaster is a catastrophe or serious misfortune.

The word came into English through French from Italian *disastro*. The second
part comes from *astro*, star. One of the earliest English senses referred to
an unfavourable influence or position of a star or planet.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

The word then settled into the broader meaning of a great misfortune or
catastrophe. The astrological belief disappeared from ordinary use, but it
remains in the word’s history.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

*The storm became a disaster for the coastal towns.*
