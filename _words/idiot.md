An idiot is a fool. The word comes from the Greek
<span lang="grc">*idiōtēs*</span>, from <span lang="grc">*idios*</span>, one's
own, private. An <span lang="grc">*idiōtēs*</span> was a private person: not in
the public eye, holding no office.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

It said nothing about intelligence. It described what a person did not take
part in. From there the word went to the common man, and from the common man to
the ignorant one, since ordinary people were not generally the ones with the
learning.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

English took it in that ignorant sense and then narrowed it again, from a man
without education to a man without sense.{% include word-cite.html n="2" %}

*Some idiot had left the gate open.*
