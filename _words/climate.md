Climate now means the usual weather conditions of a place over a long time.
The word began with an angle.{% include word-cite.html n="1,3" %}

The Greek <span lang="grc">*klima*</span> meant an inclination. Ancient
geographers divided the earth into zones by latitude and the angle of the
sun's rays. Places at similar latitudes received the noon sun at a similar
angle, and were placed in the same zone.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

Weather came to define the word more fully. Rain, wind, altitude, and local
geography also matter to a place's climate.{% include word-cite.html n="2" %}

*The island has a mild climate.*
