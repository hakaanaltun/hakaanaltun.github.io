An X-ray is radiation used to see inside things. Wilhelm Röntgen found it in
November 1895, working with a covered cathode-ray tube, and announced it in a
short paper called *On a New Kind of Rays*.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

He called them X-rays. The X is the one from algebra, the letter you put where
a quantity is not known yet. He did not know what the rays were and said so in
the name.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

German and several other languages call them Röntgen rays instead. He preferred
his own placeholder, and English kept
it.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

The rays were explained long ago. The word still says nobody
knows.{% include word-cite.html n="1" %}

*The X-ray showed a hairline fracture.*
