Quarantine is a period of separation that keeps a disease from spreading. It
comes from the Italian <span lang="it">*quaranta*</span>, forty.

During the plague, a ship arriving at Venice had to wait at anchor for forty
days before anyone could come ashore. The Italian for that period was
<span lang="it">*quarantena*</span>.

A quarantine is rarely forty days now. It is ten days, or two weeks, or
whatever the illness needs. The forty is still in the word.

*The ship was held in quarantine.*
