A mentor is someone who guides a less experienced person. The word was a
person's name first.{% include word-cite.html n="1" %}

In Homer's *Odyssey*, Mentor was a trusted friend of Odysseus. When Athena
went to help Odysseus's son Telemachus, she appeared in Mentor's form. The
advice came from the goddess, though the young man saw his father's
friend.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

Mentor took on a larger role in François Fénelon's 1699 novel *Les aventures
de Télémaque*. After that book, his name became a general word for a trusted
guide in French and then in English.{% include word-cite.html n="2" %}

*Her first editor became her mentor.*
