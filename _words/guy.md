A guy is an ordinary word for a man or, in some uses, a person.

The word comes from Guy Fawkes. Effigies of him burned on November 5 came to be
called *guys*. The word then spread to people who looked strange or badly
dressed.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

In American English, *guy* became a general word for a man, and the plural
*guys* later widened further in everyday speech.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

*He seems like a nice guy.*
