A desire is a feeling of wanting something.

English got the word through Old French from Latin *desiderare*. In Latin,
the verb could mean to long for something, but also to miss it or feel its
absence.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

There is a well-known explanation that connects *desiderare* with *sidus*,
star or constellation. On that reading, the word may once have carried an
idea connected with looking to the stars or waiting for what they would
bring.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

The exact origin is not certain. Etymologists have proposed other explanations
too, so the neat claim that *desire* simply means something like “from the
stars” goes further than the evidence allows.{% include word-cite.html n="2" %}

What is secure is the older sense of longing for what is absent.

*He had a strong desire to go home.*
