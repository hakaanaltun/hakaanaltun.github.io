An algorithm is a set of steps for solving a problem or carrying out a calculation.

The word ultimately comes from the name of the ninth-century mathematician
al-Khwarizmi. A Latin version of a work associated with him on Hindu-Arabic
numerals used a form of his name, *Algoritmi*. That name gave rise to
*algorism*, a word for arithmetic using those numerals.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

Over time, the spelling and meaning changed. *Algorithm* came to mean a
defined procedure for calculation and then, more broadly, a step-by-step
procedure for solving a problem.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}
