Influence means the power to affect someone or something. It comes from the
medieval Latin <span lang="la">*influentia*</span>, a flowing in, from
<span lang="la">*influere*</span>, to flow in.{% include word-cite.html n="1" %}

In the Middle Ages, influence was believed to be a fluid that streamed down
from the stars into people and shaped their character and their fortune.{% include word-cite.html n="1" %}

Influenza is the same word. It is simply the Italian for influence, and
epidemics were once blamed on the stars. Flu is what is left of it.{% include word-cite.html n="1" %}

*He was under the influence.*
