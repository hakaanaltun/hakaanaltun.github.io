A candidate is someone being considered for a position, office, award, or
other selection.

The word comes from Latin *candidatus*, meaning white-robed. In ancient Rome,
a man seeking public office was associated with a bright white toga, and the
word for the clothing became the word for the office-seeker.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

English later widened *candidate* beyond elections to people being considered
for many kinds of positions or outcomes.{% include word-cite.html n="1" %}

*She was one of three candidates for the position.*
