A maverick is an independent person who does not follow the group.

The word comes from Samuel A. Maverick, a nineteenth-century Texas landowner.
Unbranded cattle associated with him came to be called *mavericks*, and the
word spread through cattle country for unbranded animals.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

By the end of the nineteenth century, the word was also being used for people
who went their own way.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

*She was a maverick in her field.*
