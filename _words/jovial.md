A jovial person is cheerful and good company. The word means “of Jove,”
another name for Jupiter, the chief Roman god. It reached English from French
in the 1580s with an astrological sense: under the influence of the planet
Jupiter.{% include word-cite.html n="1" %}

Astrology held that people shared the nature of the planet that ruled their
birth. Those born under Jupiter were thought to be cheerful, and the word
kept the cheer after the belief had gone.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

It was often set against *saturnine*, gloomy. In Roman myth Saturn was
Jupiter’s father.{% include word-cite.html n="2" %}

*Our jovial host kept everyone’s glass full.*
