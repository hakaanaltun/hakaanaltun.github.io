A school is a place people are taught in. The word came through the Latin
<span lang="la">*schola*</span> from the Greek
<span lang="grc">*skholē*</span>, which meant spare time, leisure,
rest.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

The connection is less odd than it looks. Free time was what you needed in
order to sit and argue about something, so the word moved from the time to the
discussion held in it, and then to the people holding the discussion and the
place they held it in.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

Latin kept some of that width. <span lang="la">*Schola*</span> could mean
leisure for learning, a lecture, a debate, or a place of
learning.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

What came through into English is the narrowest of those: a building, a
register and a timetable.{% include word-cite.html n="1" %}

*She teaches at a school in the city.*
