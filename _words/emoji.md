An emoji is a picture character used in a message. Its name is Japanese:
<span lang="ja">*e*</span> means picture, and <span lang="ja">*moji*</span>
means character. The word has no etymological connection to the English
*emotion*.{% include word-cite.html n="1" %}

Emoji were used on Japanese mobile phones before they became familiar around
the world. Different phone companies had their own sets and their own ways
of encoding them. A symbol sent from one network could fail to appear as
intended on another.{% include word-cite.html n="2" %}

Unicode brought many of those characters into a common standard in 2010. A
word from Japanese phones now names pictures that can travel between devices
almost anywhere.{% include word-cite.html n="2" %}
