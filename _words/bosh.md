Bosh means nonsense or empty talk in English. It comes from the Turkish word
<span lang="tr">*boş*</span>, meaning empty.{% include word-cite.html n="1" %}

It became current in English in the nineteenth century through J. J. Morier’s
1834 novel *Ayesha, the Maid of Kars*. What was empty in Turkish became empty
talk in English, and then nonsense.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

It is old-fashioned now, but it is still there.

*You’re talking bosh.*
