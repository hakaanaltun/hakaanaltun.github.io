Bosh means nonsense or empty talk in English. It comes from the Turkish word
<span lang="tr">*boş*</span>, meaning empty.

It entered English in the nineteenth century and was helped along by J. J.
Morier’s 1834 novel *Ayesha, the Maid of Kars*. What was empty in Turkish
became empty talk in English, and then nonsense.

It is old-fashioned now, but it is still there.

*You’re talking bosh.*
