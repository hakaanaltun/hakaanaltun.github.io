A ballot is a vote, or the paper on which someone records one.
The word once referred to a small ball.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

Italian <span lang="it">*ballotta*</span> meant a little ball. In
Venice, small balls could be used to register votes without a public
show of hands. English borrowed the word for this way of
voting.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

Paper ballots eventually took the place of the little balls, and
the name went with them. The object changed, while the word kept
its place in an election.{% include word-cite.html n="1" %}

*The voters marked their ballots in private.*
