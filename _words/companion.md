A companion is someone who goes with you or spends time with you.

The word came through Old French from a Late Latin form built from *com-*,
together, and *panis*, bread. Its older sense was a fellow or associate
understood through the idea of sharing bread or a meal.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

The meaning widened into the familiar sense of a person who accompanies
another. The bread is no longer part of the definition, but it is part of the
word’s construction.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

*She was his companion on the long journey.*
