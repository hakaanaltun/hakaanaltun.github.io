A yacht is a boat for pleasure. The word is the Dutch
<span lang="nl">*jacht*</span>, shortened from
<span lang="nl">*jachtschip*</span>, hunting ship, from
<span lang="nl">*jagen*</span>, to hunt or
chase.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

It was built for the chase. Dutch warships were too heavy to catch the pirates
and smugglers working their coast, so lighter and faster craft were made to run
them down.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

One was presented to Charles II in 1660, and he used it to go out on the water
for the pleasure of it. English gentlemen wanted the same, and the word stopped
meaning the chase.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

*They spent August on a yacht.*
