To broadcast something is to send it out to many people.

The word was used first for scattering seed over a wide area, usually by hand.
That agricultural sense is much older than radio.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

In the early twentieth century, *broadcast* was taken up for radio transmission.
The older idea of sending something out widely was kept, but the seed had become
a signal.{% include word-cite.html n="2" %}

The agricultural meaning still exists today.{% include word-cite.html n="1" %}

*The interview was broadcast live.*
