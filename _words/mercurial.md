A mercurial person changes mood quickly and without warning. In the late
fourteenth century the word meant “of the planet Mercury,” from Latin
<span lang="la">*Mercurialis*</span>.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

Mercury was also the Roman messenger god, quick and clever, and astrology
gave his qualities to those born under his planet. By the 1640s English used
*mercurial* for a lively, quick and changeable
temperament.{% include word-cite.html n="1" %}

The metal probably helped. Mercury, or quicksilver, stays liquid and runs at
a touch, and medicine still calls anything containing it
mercurial.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

*His mercurial temper kept everyone guessing.*
