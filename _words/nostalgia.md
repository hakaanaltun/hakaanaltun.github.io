Nostalgia is a fond feeling about the past. It started as the name of an
illness.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

Johannes Hofer, a medical student at Basel, made the word for his dissertation
of 1688 out of the Greek <span lang="grc">*nostos*</span>, homecoming, and
<span lang="grc">*algos*</span>, pain. It was his Latin for the German
<span lang="de">*Heimweh*</span>, homesickness, and he described it as a
disease of the brain.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

The patients were often Swiss mercenaries serving abroad, set off by a
remembered sight or a tune from home. They stopped eating and sleeping. It was
taken seriously enough to be treated, and seriously enough to be written down
as a cause of death.{% include word-cite.html n="3" %}

The word softened as the illness stopped being one. What it kept is the ache.
The <span lang="grc">*algos*</span> is half of
it.{% include word-cite.html n="1,2" %}

*A wave of nostalgia for the nineties.*
