About this journey and its sources

This first journey stays in the Solar System. It begins near Earth, visits the Sun and all eight planets, and introduces a few of the smaller worlds. The scenes combine spacecraft images taken at different times. Their sizes and distances are adjusted to make each place possible to explore. These are arranged views, not current positions or a view from one spacecraft.

The images are observations and photographic composites. Their processing is described beside each close view, including the monochrome visible-light view of the Sun and the rebalanced colors of Uranus and Neptune. The comet is shown on a separate visit near the Sun.

Uranus and Neptune images: Patrick Irwin/University of Oxford/NASA, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (opens in a new tab). The published comparison is resized; its lower views are shown as crops.

The asteroid belt and Kuiper Belt are regions with widely separated bodies. The route shows selected examples. The arrows make large jumps between visits. Pale Blue Dot is a look back at Earth from Voyager 1 in 1990, beyond Neptune's orbit. Its close view enlarges the original pixels.

The brief Carl Sagan quotation is from Pale Blue Dot (1994). Copyright © 1994 Carl Sagan; © 2006 Democritus Properties, LLC. The linked source hosts the longer passage. The portrait is a NASA montage.
