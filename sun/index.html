Reading the sun

A height of 0° is the horizon; 90° is directly overhead. The compass bearing runs clockwise from north, through east at 90° and south at 180°. A negative height puts the sun below the horizon.

Your device’s time zone chooses a nearby city. Its coordinates stand in for yours; you can choose another city above. All calculations run in your browser, with your current clock. The height describes the sun’s center without atmospheric refraction; sunrise and sunset allow for the apparent upper edge.

Positions are approximate. Buildings, hills and the atmosphere can change what you see near the horizon.
